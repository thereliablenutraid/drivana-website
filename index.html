Privacy policy

Effective 1 January 2025 · Last updated February 2026

What we collect

  • Identity and contact details: name, date of birth, age, sex, phone, email, address and an emergency contact.
  • Health information, treated as sensitive: vital signs, medical history, medicines, allergies, pregnancy or breastfeeding status, kidney, liver or heart conditions, G6PD status, recent surgeries and clinician notes.
  • Booking and payment records: treatment history, formulations, scheduling, invoices and masked payment confirmations. We do not store raw card numbers or UPI PINs; payments go through regulated gateways.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser, device identifiers, referring pages and analytics cookies.

How we use it

  • To screen for medical suitability and set physician-supervised IV doses.
  • To assign nurses and send staff to your location.
  • To follow up after treatment and record any adverse reaction.
  • To deliver educational material and product orders.
  • To meet accounting, tax and medical record-keeping duties.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal or health data to advertisers, insurers or data brokers. We share it only as needed with assigned nurses and supervising physicians, pharmacy and diagnostic partners, technology providers under data protection agreements, and authorities when the law requires it.

Security and retention

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to medical forms is limited to licensed clinical staff involved in your care. Clinical records are kept for the minimum period required by medical guidelines and tax or accounting rules, then anonymised or deleted.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of your data and for corrections, withdraw consent to optional marketing at any time, and raise a concern with our Grievance Officer. Clinical records may be kept where retention is needed for medical safety audits.

Effective 1 January 2025 · Last updated February 2026

Scope and eligibility

These terms cover this website, at-home IV sessions, aesthetic and wellness consultations, and products delivered in Bangalore and Karnataka. You must be at least 18 and able to contract under the Indian Contract Act, 1872.

Booking and dispatch

We send registered nurses to your home or office in Bangalore. Please provide a clean, well-lit, ventilated and safe space for the visiting clinician.

Medical screening and consent

Please tell us about all medicines, supplements, allergies and conditions, including kidney, heart, liver, blood pressure and G6PD-related issues, pregnancy, breastfeeding and recent surgeries. If medical information is withheld or misstated, Drivana and its staff are released from resulting liability. Clinicians may refuse, change or stop any treatment if vital signs are abnormal, contraindications appear, or the client appears intoxicated, under the influence of illicit substances or uncooperative.

Not an emergency service

Drivana is not an emergency provider and does not replace your primary doctor or a specialist. In an emergency, call 112 or 108, or go to the nearest hospital emergency department.

Procedural risks

IV and IM procedures carry known risks, including pain at the insertion site, bruising, dizziness or fainting, vein irritation, fluid leaking into surrounding tissue, and rare allergic reactions to vitamins. By consenting, you accept these risks.

Cancellation and fees

Please ask to cancel or reschedule at least 3 hours before your appointment. If you cancel after a nurse has been dispatched, or within 2 hours of the slot, a travel or dispatch fee of ₹500 may apply. A no-show, or a session stopped because of intoxication or abusive behaviour, is treated as rendered and non-refundable.

Conduct

Harassment, abuse or intimidation towards staff ends the session immediately without refund and may be reported to law enforcement.

Products

Use products as the packaging directs. Do a 24-hour patch test before applying a topical product, and stop if irritation occurs.
